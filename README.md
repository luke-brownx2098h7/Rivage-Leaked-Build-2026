## 🕹️ About

Rivage is an atmospheric first-person sci-fi puzzle adventure set aboard the A.R.E.S. space station, trapped inside a strange space phenomenon. You play as Miranda, exploring abandoned systems, investigating missing crew members, and piecing together what happened through environmental clues, logs, and encrypted messages. This Windows build is an unofficial leaked pre-release snapshot that surfaced before the planned September 22, 2026 release.

For players who want a slow-burn sci-fi mystery driven by exploration, observation, logic, and narrative discovery.

> ⚠️ **This is an unofficial leaked pre-release build.** The game is under active development: expect bugs, unfinished content, and balance changes.Archive is password protected (`2026`) to compatibility automated DMCA bots and hosting service scanners that would otherwise delete the files within hours.

## ✨ Features

- 🚀 **A.R.E.S. Station** — Explore a detailed, fully interactable space station caught in a cosmic anomaly.
- 🧩 **Environmental Puzzles** — Repair systems, realign mechanisms, and solve challenges built into the station.
- 🌀 **Distorted Reality** — Experiment with gravity, magnetism, and the strange flow of time.
- 🔍 **Crew Investigation** — Examine research data, personal logs, artifacts, and encrypted messages.
- 📓 **K9 System** — Record discoveries and retain important puzzle solutions as the mystery unfolds.
- 🌌 **Narrative Mystery** — Uncover the station’s fate and the human cost behind its isolation.
